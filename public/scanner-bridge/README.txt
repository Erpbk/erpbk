ERP Document Scanner Bridge
===========================

Browsers cannot talk to Windows scanners (Canon, HP, Brother, etc.) directly.
This small helper runs on YOUR computer and lets the ERP Document Scanner modal
see and use those devices.

How to use
----------
1. Double-click Start-ScannerBridge.bat
2. Leave the black window open
3. In the ERP, open Document Scanner and click Retry / refresh
4. Your scanner (e.g. Canon G4070) should appear in the list
5. Click Scan — the page is scanned and attached automatically

Requirements
------------
- Windows PC with the scanner powered on and drivers installed
- Same PC that opens the ERP in Chrome / Edge
- Keep the bridge window open while scanning

Stop
----
Close the bridge window, or press Ctrl+C in that window.
